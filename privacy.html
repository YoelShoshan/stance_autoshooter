Privacy Policy — EDGELORD

Last updated: July 2026

EDGELORD ("the game", "we") is a single-player arcade game. This policy explains what data the game does and does not handle. In short: the game does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data.

Data we collect

None. EDGELORD does not collect personal information. There are no user accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking. We do not know who you are.

Data stored on your device

The game saves progress locally on your own device using your browser's localStorage. This includes: which levels you have unlocked, your best score, and your sound/music preferences. This information never leaves your device, is not transmitted to us or anyone else, and can be cleared at any time by clearing the app's site data.

Network use

The game loads its files from its web address and may cache them for offline play using a service worker. It does not send any of your information over the network during play.

Children

EDGELORD does not collect data from anyone, including children, and contains no ads or purchases.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted at this URL with a new "last updated" date.
